Saudi E-Invoice System — ZATCA Compliant

Regulatory compliance tool · Free public utility

A complete e-invoicing platform that lets any Saudi business issue legally valid tax invoices in seconds. It captures the seller's VAT registration details, calculates 15% VAT across unlimited line items, and generates the ZATCA-compliant QR code — verifiable directly in the official ZATCA app — then exports a print-ready invoice or PDF.

Why it matters Non-compliant invoicing carries real penalties in Saudi Arabia, and most small businesses can't afford enterprise ERP software. This tool removes that barrier entirely — zero cost, zero setup, fully compliant output. It's proof I can take a dense government regulation and ship it as a tool people actually use.

High Pressure Cables Factory — Corporate Platform

Commercial website · Manufacturer, Riyadh

A full bilingual (English / Arabic) platform for a Saudi cable and wire manufacturer. It presents five complete product families — flexible cords, flat cables, building wires, THHN/THWN and bare copper conductors — alongside certifications against Saudi, IEC and British standards, and a quote request flow that turns visitors into qualified leads.

Why it matters Industrial buyers evaluate suppliers on credibility before price. The site was built to answer that: standards and certifications up front, clear technical product data, and a direct path to a quote — giving the factory a serious commercial presence in both of its markets.
