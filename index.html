01Flagship · Independent product

Inglish

A platform for passing the STEP exam and learning English, built Arabic-first.

Role
Everything: product, content, design, backend, operations
Type
Full-stack web app · Installable PWA
Stack
JavaScript · Vercel Functions · Redis
Status
Live — step-english-lime.vercel.app

STEP scores decide university admission and scholarships in Saudi Arabia, yet most students prepare from scattered PDFs with no feedback. Inglish turns that into one guided path, and gives the whole family a reason to come back every day.

ALearn

Two tracks: STEP preparation, and general English from A1 to B2. Short units pair vocabulary with a plain-language grammar lesson, a spoken dialogue and a 12-question test.

BPractise

Smart training pulls from your weakest topics and brings mistakes back with spaced repetition. A timed exam mode mirrors the real test and can be sent to a friend as a challenge link.

CCompete

Accounts, weekly and monthly leaderboards, daily streaks with shields, a golden hour for double points and a live Friday word battle, with push reminders before each one.

Engineering notes

01Read-along audio

Narration is generated offline, with a slower, clearer voice for beginners and natural speed at C2. Each sentence is stored as [start, end, paragraph, from, to], so the text lights up in sync.

02A points ledger that always adds up

Every point is written under its source (vocabulary, stories, events, rewards), so the total always equals the breakdown. A script audits it against the database.

03Fair by design

A question pays out once, the first time it's answered correctly. The Friday battle is seeded by date, so every player gets the same 60 seconds of words.

04Small, fast backend

11 serverless endpoints on Vercel with Redis, scrypt password hashing and signed sessions, all running on a single npm dependency.

05Content that's tested like code

Scripts check that every structure a grammar quiz tests is actually taught in its lesson, and that vocabulary and points data stay consistent.

06Installs like an app

A PWA with a service worker, a home-screen icon and web push notifications, plus speech recognition for speaking practice.

§2About

Before I built tools for regulated businesses, I spent 29 months working inside one.

At the General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) I handled insurance records and beneficiary processes, where a wrong record isn't a bug, it's a liability. That's where I learned how compliance, documentation and approvals actually work.

Before that, sales and marketing taught me to judge work by the value it creates, not the features it has. The Tuwaiq Academy game development bootcamp gave me the engineering discipline to back it up.

Today I manage a manufacturer's website end-to-end, and build my own products alongside it. The biggest is Inglish, which I design, write, build and run myself.

How I work
01

Start from the rule, not the stack.

Read the regulation, the exam or the buyer's checklist first. The technology follows from that.

02

Ship, then operate.

Launch is the halfway point. Products are won on the performance, content and data work that comes after it.

03

Arabic is not a translation.

Right-to-left layout, typography and tone are designed in from day one, not patched on at the end.

04

Make it verifiable.

Totals that reconcile, QR codes that scan, content that's checked by scripts. Trust is a feature.
